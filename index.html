About Us

Zerca’s SIMPLSYA Grill & Restaurant is a rustic, welcoming dining space in Laguna that embodies the Filipino value of simplicity—serving straightforward, delicious food and drinks designed for family and friends to enjoy together. Below is a polished overview, mission, and vision statement crafted for website use.

🌟 Our Story

Zerca’s SIMPLSYA Grill & Restaurant is built on the philosophy of simplicity with elegance. The name SIMPLSYA reflects our vision: creating a dining experience that is easy, relaxed, and memorable.

Location: Cabuyao, Laguna, Philippines

Services: Grazing Table, Mobile Bar, Restaurant Dining

Ambiance: Rustic, cozy, and family-friendly

Specialty: Simple yet flavorful Filipino-inspired dishes, complemented by refreshing beverages

Our restaurant is more than a place to eat—it’s a space where people gather, celebrate, and enjoy life’s simple pleasures.

🎯 Our Mission

  • Serving delicious, freshly prepared food that highlights Filipino culinary traditions
  • Creating a relaxed environment where families and friends can connect
  • Offering professional catering services (grazing tables and mobile bars) that bring our rustic charm to any event
  • Ensuring quality, affordability, and consistency in every dish and service we provide

✨ Our Vision

  • A restaurant that celebrates Filipino hospitality through food and service
  • Expanding our reach by offering mobile bar and grazing table services to more communities
  • Becoming a trusted brand in the local dining scene, known for balancing elegance with simplicity
  • Building lasting relationships with customers who value authentic, memorable dining experiences
